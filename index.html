Who We Are

Meeple Center was founded by a small group of local players who wanted a regular, welcoming place to push cardboard together in Mower County. What started as a few folks around a kitchen table has grown into an all-volunteer, 501(c)(7) non-profit organization serving the wider Austin community.

Our mission is simple: make tabletop gaming accessible to everyone in the area, regardless of experience, budget, or background. We believe a board game is one of the friendliest ways to meet your neighbors.

No experience is necessary. Teachers are on hand at every meetup to walk newcomers through the rules, no matter how heavy or light the game.

What We Do

Weekly Game Nights

Drop-in game nights every week, open to members and newcomers alike. Just show up and find a table.

A Lending Library

More than 150 games members can borrow and take home. Browse the full game catalog.

Learn-to-Play Tables

Tables staffed by volunteers who love teaching, from quick fillers to weighty strategy titles.
