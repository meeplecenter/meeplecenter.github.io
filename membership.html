Membership Levels

Please reach out if none of these options fit your budget. We want to help you join our community!

Level Cost Game Rentals Event Access Includes Household Extras
Rogue $2/mo Support the community from the shadows. Rogue dues buy new games and keep the rental library growing.
Wizard Most popular $3/mo Our highest monthly level. Steady Wizard dues stabilize our revenue and let us host public events.
Fighter $15/yr One payment a year, no monthly commitment. A discounted option for regulars at Friday Game Nights.

How to Sign Up

Four steps, in order. The whole thing takes about two minutes.

  1. Open the online membership form.
  2. Select the membership level that best fits your needs.
  3. Optionally, enter contact information to get event reminders.
  4. Submit your payment information and you're ready to go!

Code of Conduct

A few plain-language ground rules:

  • Be welcoming: introduce yourself and help newcomers find a game.
  • Take care of library games: be cautious with food or drink near the components, and report any damage.
  • No harassment of any kind. We have zero tolerance for it.
  • We use shared spaces. Please be respectful to it and others using the space.
