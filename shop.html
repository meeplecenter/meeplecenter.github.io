Before You Buy

Sign In First

The shop is members-only. Sign in to Zeffy with the email your membership is under, and the items unlock.

Minnesota Only

For now, we can only ship to addresses in Minnesota. Reach out if you'd like to ship elsewhere!

Order Processing

We send orders to our printer every Tuesday morning. This helps us accommodate our payment processing. Thank you for your patience!

Tracking by Email

When your order ships, you'll get one email with tracking from shipping@meeplecenter.org. Check your junk folder if it hasn't arrived.

How Long It Takes

Your order goes to our printer on Tuesday. From there, allow about a week for it to be processed, printed, and delivered, so one to two weeks in all.

Tracking says UPS, but the last stretch is often USPS, so it may arrive with your regular mail.

Questions

Questions about the shop or an order? Email meeplecenter@gmail.com, or reply to your tracking email.

Privacy Policy

We keep your shipping address until 30 days after delivery, then erase it. Zeffy handles your payment details.

Ready to Shop?

Sign in with your membership email, pick your size, and we'll take it from there.
